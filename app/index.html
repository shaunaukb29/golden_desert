What Can This Engine Sound Diagnostic AI App Detect?

The same range of mechanical faults a dedicated diagnostic app would cover, matched against known acoustic signatures:

  • Ticking or tapping — often lifter, valvetrain, or injector-related
  • Knocking — commonly rod bearing wear or detonation under load
  • Rattling — loose heat shields, brackets, or interior trim
  • Whining — power steering pump, alternator, transmission, or wheel bearing
  • Squealing — worn or glazed serpentine belt, loose tensioner
  • Grinding — worn brake pads or transmission synchronizer issues
