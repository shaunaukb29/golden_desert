How the Car Noise Identifier Works

01
Acoustic Sampling
Captures frequency spectrums between 20 Hz and 20,000 Hz while filtering out background ambient wind and road noise.
02
Pattern Matching
Cross-references isolated sound waves against categorized mechanical failure signatures in our diagnostic database.
03
Fault Pinpointing
Calculates probability metrics across major sub-systems: belts, valvetrain, wheel end assemblies, braking systems, and exhaust.
