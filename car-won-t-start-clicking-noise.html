Car won't start clicking noise

Carithm AI Diagnosis

If your car won’t start and you hear a rapid or single clicking noise, the most common causes include:

  • Weak or dead battery – the starter isn’t getting enough power.
  • Bad starter motor – the solenoid clicks but the motor doesn’t engage.
  • Corroded battery terminals – poor connection reduces current flow.
  • Alternator failure – battery not charging properly.

Carithm AI suggests checking the battery voltage first (should be around 12.6V fully charged). If the battery is fine, test the starter and alternator. Jump-starting the car can also help diagnose if it’s a battery issue.
