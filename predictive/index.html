🧩 Predictive Maintenance AI

Experimental Demo – Full App Coming Soon 🚀

Carithm's Predictive Maintenance AI helps forecast when car parts might fail. This is a free demo – a full mobile + desktop app will launch soon.

To use the demo, simply input:

  • Car type (SUV, Sedan, Hatchback, etc.)
  • Mileage
  • Vehicle age (years)
  • Months since last service
  • Driving environment (hot, cold, dusty, etc.)
  • Driving style (city, highway, spirited)
  • Country of car origin

The AI will generate probable failure timelines and maintenance forecasts.
