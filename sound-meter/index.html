More Than Just a Noise Level Meter

Our online sound level meter extracts deep acoustic intelligence from a standard microphone. Here is the information our recording software reveals about your audio environment:

LUFS & Loudness

We implement the exact ITU-R BS.1770-4 standard used by Netflix and Spotify. View Momentary, Short-term, and Integrated LUFS with K-weighted precision.

True Peak Detection

Digital peaks can hide between samples. Our engine uses 4x polyphase sinc interpolation to catch inter-sample clipping (dBTP) that standard meters miss.

Spectral Analysis & RTA

Visualize the frequency domain live. See the Spectral Centroid (brightness), Spectral Flatness (tonality), and a 31-band 1/3-Octave Real-Time Analyzer.

Live Spectrogram

Map time, frequency, and amplitude on a stunning heatmap. Perfect for spotting resonant hums, mechanical whine, or intermittent squeals.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does an online sound meter work?

Your browser asks permission to use your microphone. Once allowed, our advanced Web Audio DSP engine measures the incoming sound many times per second, converting it to precise decibel (dB) and LUFS readings, alongside rich spectral charts.

Is this online decibel meter accurate?

It is highly accurate for relative comparisons, acoustic diagnostics, and general environmental awareness. However, because built-in device microphones are not factory-calibrated, absolute dB values may vary. For strict legal compliance, a Class 1 meter is required.

Can this app diagnose my car or washing machine?

Yes! Carithm specializes in Acoustic AI Diagnostics. Alongside measuring the decibel level, our broader platform analyzes the frequency signature of car engines, HVAC systems, and washing machines to detect mechanical faults.

Does the sound meter record or store my audio?

No. Sound is measured live in your browser and nothing is recorded, uploaded to our servers, or saved. Closing the tab immediately clears the readings.
