Wheel Alignment Cost in the UAE (2026)

Proper wheel alignment keeps your vehicle driving straight, improves tyre life, and helps maintain safe handling on UAE roads.

What Is Wheel Alignment?

Wheel alignment is the process of adjusting the suspension angles so that all four wheels are positioned according to the manufacturer's specifications. Correct alignment improves steering accuracy, tyre longevity, fuel efficiency, and overall driving stability.

Signs Your Vehicle Needs a Wheel Alignment

  • Vehicle pulls to one side while driving
  • Steering wheel is off-centre
  • Uneven or rapid tyre wear
  • Steering feels unstable at highway speeds
  • Squealing tyres when turning
  • Reduced fuel efficiency due to increased rolling resistance

Wheel Alignment Cost (UAE 2026)

Wheel alignment typically costs between AED 100 and AED 350 depending on the vehicle type, whether a two-wheel or four-wheel alignment is required, and the workshop's equipment.

When Should You Get a Wheel Alignment?

It's recommended to check wheel alignment every 10,000–20,000 km, after replacing suspension or steering components, installing new tyres, or after hitting large potholes or kerbs.

Benefits of Proper Wheel Alignment

  • Longer tyre lifespan
  • Improved steering response
  • Better fuel economy
  • More stable handling
  • Reduced suspension wear
