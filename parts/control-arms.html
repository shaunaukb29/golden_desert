Toyota Camry Control Arm Replacement in UAE (2026)

The control arm is a key suspension component in the Toyota Camry that connects the wheel hub to the chassis and allows controlled up-and-down movement. In UAE driving conditions, rough roads, speed bumps, and heat stress can cause bushings and joints to wear faster.

What Control Arms Do

Control arms help maintain wheel alignment and stability while driving. They allow smooth suspension movement while keeping the tires properly connected to the road surface.

Common Signs of Worn Control Arms

  • Clunking or knocking noise over bumps
  • Uneven tire wear
  • Steering feels loose or unstable
  • Vibration in steering wheel at higher speeds
  • Car pulls slightly to one side

Control Arm Replacement Cost in UAE (2026)

Costs vary depending on whether only bushings are replaced or the full control arm assembly is changed.

  • Control arm bushing replacement: moderate cost
  • Full control arm replacement: higher cost
  • Front control arms: usually more expensive than rear
  • Wheel alignment after replacement: additional cost

When Should You Replace It?

Control arms should be inspected when suspension noise appears or during alignment issues. If bushings are cracked or ball joints are loose, replacement is necessary for safety.

Recommended Fix

In most cases, replacing the full control arm assembly is more reliable than replacing individual bushings, especially in high-heat and rough-road conditions like the UAE.

Preventive Maintenance Tips

Avoid driving aggressively over potholes and speed bumps. Regular suspension checks and timely wheel alignment help extend control arm lifespan.
