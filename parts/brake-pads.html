Toyota Camry Brake Pad Replacement in UAE (2026)

Brake pads are one of the most frequently replaced components in the Toyota Camry braking system. In UAE driving conditions, heavy traffic and frequent stop-and-go driving cause brake pads to wear faster than usual.

What Brake Pads Do

Brake pads create friction against the brake discs (rotors) when you press the brake pedal. This friction slows down the wheels and brings the vehicle to a controlled stop.

Common Signs of Worn Brake Pads

  • Squeaking or squealing noise when braking
  • Grinding sound (metal-on-metal contact)
  • Longer stopping distance
  • Brake warning light on dashboard (in some cases)
  • Thin or visibly worn pad material

Brake Pad Replacement Cost in UAE (2026)

Costs depend on pad quality (OEM vs aftermarket) and whether front, rear, or full set is replaced.

  • Front brake pads: moderate cost
  • Rear brake pads: slightly lower cost than front
  • OEM brake pads: higher cost but better durability
  • Aftermarket pads: lower cost but variable performance
  • Labor: usually moderate depending on workshop

When Should You Replace Brake Pads?

Brake pads should typically be replaced when thickness reaches the minimum safe level or when noise and braking performance issues appear. Most drivers in UAE replace pads more frequently due to traffic conditions.

Recommended Fix

Always inspect brake pads together with brake discs. Replacing pads early helps prevent damage to rotors and avoids costly repairs later.

Preventive Maintenance Tips

Avoid harsh braking, maintain steady driving in traffic when possible, and schedule regular brake inspections during routine servicing.
