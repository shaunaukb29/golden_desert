Transmission Repair Cost in UAE (2026)

The transmission transfers engine power to the wheels and allows your vehicle to change gears smoothly for efficient performance.

What Does the Transmission Do?

The transmission works with the engine to deliver the correct amount of power at different speeds. Whether automatic or manual, it helps improve fuel economy, acceleration, and overall driving comfort.

Common Signs of Transmission Problems

  • Delayed or harsh gear shifts
  • Slipping gears while driving
  • Transmission fluid leaks
  • Grinding, whining, or humming noises
  • Burning smell or warning lights on the dashboard

Transmission Repair Cost (UAE 2026)

Transmission repairs typically range from AED 800 to AED 8,000 depending on the severity of the problem. Complete transmission replacement or rebuilding can cost significantly more than minor repairs such as replacing seals, sensors, or fluid.

How to Extend Transmission Life

Regular transmission fluid changes, avoiding excessive towing, and addressing shifting problems early can greatly extend the lifespan of your transmission and prevent expensive repairs.

Conclusion

The transmission is one of the most complex and expensive components in any vehicle. Routine maintenance and early diagnosis of transmission issues help ensure smooth performance and reduce long-term repair costs.
