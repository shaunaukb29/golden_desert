Oil Filter Replacement Cost in UAE (2026)

The oil filter removes dirt and metal particles from engine oil to keep your engine clean and protected.

What Does an Oil Filter Do?

An oil filter traps contaminants from engine oil, ensuring only clean oil circulates through the engine’s internal components.

Signs of a Clogged Oil Filter

  • Reduced engine performance
  • Dirty or dark engine oil
  • Engine warning light on dashboard
  • Unusual engine noise or ticking

Oil Filter Replacement Cost (UAE 2026)

Oil filter replacement usually costs between AED 40 – AED 150, often included with regular oil service.

When Should You Replace It?

Oil filters should typically be replaced every oil change, around 5,000 – 10,000 km depending on oil type and driving conditions.

Conclusion

Regular oil filter replacement protects your engine from contamination and extends overall engine life.
