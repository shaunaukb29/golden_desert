Timing Chain Replacement Cost in UAE (2026)

The timing chain synchronizes the crankshaft and camshaft, ensuring the engine's valves and pistons operate in precise timing.

What Does a Timing Chain Do?

Unlike a timing belt, a timing chain is made of metal and is lubricated by engine oil. It is designed for long-term durability while maintaining accurate valve timing for smooth engine performance, fuel efficiency, and reliable power delivery.

Common Signs of Timing Chain Problems

  • Rattling noise from the front of the engine, especially during startup
  • Check Engine light is illuminated
  • Engine misfires or rough idle
  • Reduced engine performance or poor acceleration
  • Metal particles found during an engine oil change

Timing Chain Replacement Cost (UAE 2026)

Timing chain replacement typically costs between AED 1,500 and AED 5,000 depending on the vehicle model, engine configuration, and labor required to access the chain assembly.

When Should a Timing Chain Be Replaced?

Many timing chains are designed to last the lifetime of the engine, but poor maintenance or infrequent oil changes can accelerate wear. If unusual engine noises or timing-related faults appear, the chain and tensioners should be inspected immediately.

Conclusion

A properly maintained timing chain helps protect your engine from serious mechanical damage. Regular oil changes and prompt attention to warning signs can significantly extend its service life and reduce repair costs.
