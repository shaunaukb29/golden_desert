Timing Belt Replacement Cost in UAE (2026)

The timing belt synchronizes the engine's crankshaft and camshaft, ensuring the valves and pistons move in perfect harmony.

What Does a Timing Belt Do?

The timing belt keeps the engine's internal components precisely synchronized. This allows the intake and exhaust valves to open and close at the correct time during each combustion cycle, helping the engine run efficiently and smoothly.

Signs of a Worn Timing Belt

  • Ticking noise coming from the engine
  • Difficulty starting the vehicle
  • Engine misfires or rough running
  • Loss of engine power or poor acceleration
  • Visible cracks or wear on the belt during inspection

Timing Belt Replacement Cost (UAE 2026)

Replacing a timing belt generally costs between AED 800 and AED 3,000, depending on the vehicle, engine design, and whether related components such as the water pump and tensioners are replaced at the same time.

When Should You Replace It?

Most manufacturers recommend replacing the timing belt every 60,000–100,000 km or according to the vehicle's maintenance schedule. Delaying replacement can lead to severe engine damage if the belt fails unexpectedly.

Conclusion

The timing belt is one of the most critical maintenance items in many engines. Replacing it on schedule helps avoid expensive repairs and keeps your vehicle operating reliably.
