Drive Belt Replacement Cost in UAE (2026)

Drive belts power essential engine components like the alternator, air conditioning compressor, and power steering system.

What Are Drive Belts?

Drive belts, also known as serpentine or auxiliary belts, transfer engine power to multiple accessories that keep your car running smoothly.

Common Drive Belt Problems

  • Squealing noise from engine bay
  • Cracks or visible wear on the belt
  • Loss of power steering assist
  • Battery warning light due to alternator issues

Drive Belt Replacement Cost (UAE 2026)

Replacement costs typically range between AED 150 – AED 600 depending on belt type and labor charges.

When Should You Replace Drive Belts?

Drive belts should be replaced every 60,000 – 100,000 km or immediately if cracks, glazing, or noise appear.

Conclusion

Healthy drive belts ensure all engine accessories function correctly and prevent sudden breakdowns.
