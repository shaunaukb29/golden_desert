Wheel Bearing Replacement Cost in the UAE (2026)

Wheel bearings allow the wheels to rotate smoothly with minimal friction while supporting the vehicle's weight. When they wear out, they can affect handling, tyre wear, and overall driving safety.

What Does a Wheel Bearing Do?

A wheel bearing is a sealed assembly of precision steel balls or rollers that sits inside the wheel hub. It enables the wheel to spin freely while carrying the load of the vehicle, helping ensure smooth steering, stable handling, and efficient operation.

Common Signs of a Worn Wheel Bearing

  • Humming, grinding, or growling noise that increases with speed
  • Steering vibration or wheel wobble
  • Uneven tyre wear
  • ABS warning light on some vehicles
  • Loose or unstable steering feel
  • Noise becomes louder when turning

Wheel Bearing Replacement Cost (UAE 2026)

Replacing a wheel bearing generally costs between AED 350 and AED 1,500 per wheel. The final price depends on the vehicle, whether the bearing is integrated into the hub assembly, and the labour required for installation.

What Causes Wheel Bearing Failure?

  • Normal wear over high mileage
  • Impact damage from potholes or kerbs
  • Water or dirt contamination
  • Improper installation or incorrect torque
  • Driving with damaged suspension components

Conclusion

Ignoring a noisy wheel bearing can lead to poor handling, accelerated tyre wear, and additional damage to the wheel hub.
