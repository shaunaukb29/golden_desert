Radiator Replacement Cost in UAE (2026)

The radiator is a key part of your car’s cooling system, responsible for preventing engine overheating.

What Does a Radiator Do?

The radiator removes heat from engine coolant, helping maintain optimal engine temperature during driving.

Common Radiator Problems

  • Engine overheating
  • Coolant leaks under the car
  • Rust or corrosion on radiator fins
  • Low coolant levels frequently
  • Steam coming from engine bay

Radiator Replacement Cost (UAE 2026)

Radiator replacement costs typically range between AED 500 – AED 2,500 depending on vehicle model and part quality.

When Should You Replace It?

You should replace the radiator if there are persistent leaks, overheating issues, or visible damage to the cooling fins or core.

Conclusion

A properly functioning radiator is essential for engine health and prevents costly overheating damage.
