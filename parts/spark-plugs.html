Spark Plug Replacement Cost in UAE (2026)

Spark plugs ignite the air-fuel mixture inside the engine, enabling combustion and smooth engine operation.

What Do Spark Plugs Do?

Spark plugs create an electrical spark that ignites fuel in the engine cylinders, powering your vehicle.

Signs of Bad Spark Plugs

  • Engine misfires or rough idling
  • Poor fuel economy
  • Slow acceleration
  • Difficulty starting the engine
  • Check engine light is on

Spark Plug Replacement Cost (UAE 2026)

Replacement costs typically range between AED 120 – AED 600 depending on engine type and spark plug quality.

When Should You Replace Them?

Spark plugs should generally be replaced every 30,000 – 100,000 km depending on material type (copper, platinum, iridium).

Conclusion

Fresh spark plugs improve engine performance, fuel efficiency, and ensure smooth ignition every time you start your car.
