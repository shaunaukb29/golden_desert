Known Toyota Camry Issues (2018-2024)

AC Compressor Wear

Continuous highway use in summer heat accelerates compressor wear, typically surfacing after 80,000-120,000 km.

CVT/Automatic Transmission Shudder

Some model years report mild shudder under light acceleration; a transmission fluid flush often resolves early symptoms.

Rear Suspension Bushings

Bushings degrade faster on Sheikh Zayed Road-heavy commutes due to sustained high speed and heat.

Dealer vs Independent Garage Pricing

Dealer servicing is typically 30–60% more expensive than experienced independent workshops in the UAE. However, the lowest quote isn't always the best value. Labour quality, genuine parts availability, and workshop expertise should all be considered when comparing repair estimates.

Why Costs Vary

Repair costs rarely depend on the failed component alone. Vehicle age, mileage, previous maintenance, workshop labour rates, genuine versus aftermarket parts, and the demanding GCC climate all influence what you'll ultimately pay.
