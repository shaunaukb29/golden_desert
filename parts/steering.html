Steering System Repair Cost in UAE (2026)

The steering system allows you to control your vehicle’s direction safely and precisely.

What Does the Steering System Do?

The steering system converts driver input into wheel movement, allowing the car to turn smoothly and accurately.

Signs of Steering Problems

  • Steering wheel feels stiff or hard to turn
  • Loose or unresponsive steering
  • Unusual noises when turning
  • Car pulls to one side
  • Fluid leaks under the vehicle

Steering Repair Cost (UAE 2026)

Repair costs typically range between AED 300 – AED 2,500 depending on whether it is a minor adjustment or major component replacement.

When Should You Service It?

Steering systems should be inspected immediately if you notice stiffness, vibration, or loss of control while driving.

Conclusion

A properly functioning steering system is critical for safe driving, vehicle control, and road stability.
