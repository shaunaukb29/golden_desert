Engine Oil Change Cost in UAE (2026)

Engine oil keeps your engine lubricated, cool, and protected from wear and friction.

What Does Engine Oil Do?

Engine oil reduces friction between moving parts, prevents overheating, and helps remove dirt and debris inside the engine.

Types of Engine Oil

  • Mineral Oil: Basic protection for older engines
  • Semi-Synthetic Oil: Balanced performance and cost
  • Fully Synthetic Oil: Best protection for modern engines

Signs You Need an Oil Change

  • Dark, dirty oil on dipstick
  • Engine noise or knocking sound
  • Oil warning light on dashboard
  • Reduced fuel efficiency

Engine Oil Change Cost (UAE 2026)

Oil change service typically costs between AED 120 – AED 450 depending on oil type and vehicle engine size.

When Should You Change Engine Oil?

Most vehicles require an oil change every 5,000 – 10,000 km depending on driving conditions and oil type.

Conclusion

Regular oil changes are one of the most important maintenance tasks to ensure long engine life and smooth performance.
