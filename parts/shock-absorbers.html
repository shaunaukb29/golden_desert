Shock Absorber Replacement Cost in UAE (2026)

Shock absorbers control vehicle bounce and ensure a smooth, stable ride over uneven roads.

What Do Shock Absorbers Do?

Shock absorbers reduce suspension movement by dampening road impact, improving comfort, stability, and tire contact with the road.

Signs of Worn Shock Absorbers

  • Excessive bouncing after hitting bumps
  • Vehicle nose-diving during braking
  • Uneven or rapid tire wear
  • Instability while cornering
  • Oil leakage on shock body

Shock Absorber Replacement Cost (UAE 2026)

Replacement cost typically ranges between AED 300 – AED 1,500 per pair depending on vehicle type and brand quality.

When Should You Replace Them?

Shock absorbers should usually be replaced every 60,000 – 100,000 km or earlier if handling or ride quality declines.

Conclusion

Healthy shock absorbers ensure safe braking, stable handling, and a comfortable driving experience.
