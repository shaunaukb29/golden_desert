CV Axles: Symptoms, Repair Cost & Replacement Guide (UAE 2026)

What Are CV Axles?

Constant Velocity (CV) axles transfer power from your vehicle’s transmission to the wheels while allowing smooth steering and suspension movement. They are essential for front-wheel and all-wheel-drive vehicles.

Common Signs of a Failing CV Axle

  • Clicking or popping sound when turning
  • Vibration while accelerating
  • Grease leaking near tires
  • Shuddering during driving at low speeds
  • Knocking noise under load

CV Axle Replacement Cost in UAE (2026)

The cost of CV axle replacement in the UAE depends on vehicle model and part quality. On average:

  • Economy cars: AED 350 – AED 700 per axle
  • Mid-range vehicles: AED 600 – AED 1,200
  • Premium vehicles: AED 1,200 – AED 2,500+

Labor charges may vary depending on workshop location and suspension condition.

Can You Drive With a Bad CV Axle?

Driving with a damaged CV axle is risky. A severely worn axle can break completely, causing loss of power to the wheels and potentially leaving the vehicle undrivable.

When Should You Replace It?

CV axles should be replaced immediately once you notice persistent clicking noises, torn CV boots, or vibration during acceleration. Delaying repair can lead to transmission stress and higher repair costs.

Maintenance Tips

  • Inspect CV boots during every oil change
  • Avoid aggressive acceleration on full steering lock
  • Replace damaged boots immediately to prevent contamination
  • Check suspension alignment regularly
