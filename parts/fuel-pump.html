Fuel Pump Replacement Cost in UAE (2026)

The fuel pump is responsible for delivering fuel from the tank to the engine at the correct pressure.

What Does a Fuel Pump Do?

The fuel pump ensures a steady supply of fuel to the engine, allowing proper combustion and smooth performance.

Signs of a Failing Fuel Pump

  • Engine struggles to start
  • Loss of power during acceleration
  • Sputtering at high speeds
  • Engine stalling unexpectedly
  • Whining noise from fuel tank area

Fuel Pump Replacement Cost (UAE 2026)

Fuel pump replacement typically costs between AED 500 – AED 2,000 depending on vehicle type and pump quality.

When Should You Replace It?

You should replace the fuel pump immediately if the engine stalls frequently, struggles to start, or loses consistent fuel pressure.

Conclusion

A healthy fuel pump ensures smooth engine operation, reliable starting, and consistent fuel delivery under all driving conditions.
