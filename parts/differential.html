Differential Repair Cost in UAE (2026)

The differential helps wheels rotate at different speeds for smooth cornering and stable handling.

What Is a Differential?

The differential is a gearbox between the drive wheels that allows them to rotate at different speeds while turning.

Symptoms of Differential Problems

  • Whining or humming noise while driving
  • Vibration during acceleration
  • Difficulty turning smoothly
  • Leaking gear oil under the car

Differential Repair Cost (UAE 2026)

Typical repair or replacement cost ranges from AED 800 – AED 3,500 depending on damage severity and vehicle type.

Why Differential Health Matters

A failing differential can severely impact steering stability, tire wear, and overall driving safety.

Conclusion

Regular maintenance and oil checks can extend differential life and prevent costly drivetrain repairs.
