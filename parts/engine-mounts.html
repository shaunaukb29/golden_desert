Engine Mount Replacement Cost in UAE (2026)

Engine mounts secure the engine to the chassis and reduce vibration while driving.

What Are Engine Mounts?

Engine mounts are rubber and metal components that hold the engine in place while absorbing vibrations and shocks from the road and engine movement.

Signs of Bad Engine Mounts

  • Excessive vibration inside the cabin
  • Clunking noise during acceleration or braking
  • Engine movement when shifting gears
  • Visible cracks or separation in rubber mounts

Engine Mount Replacement Cost (UAE 2026)

Replacement costs usually range from AED 300 – AED 1,200 depending on vehicle model and number of mounts replaced.

When Should You Replace Engine Mounts?

Engine mounts typically last 5–7 years but should be replaced earlier if vibration or noise becomes noticeable.

Conclusion

Replacing worn engine mounts improves driving comfort, reduces vibration, and protects engine alignment.
