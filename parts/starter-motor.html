Starter Motor Replacement Cost in UAE (2026)

The starter motor is responsible for cranking your engine when you turn the ignition key or press the start button.

What Does a Starter Motor Do?

The starter motor uses electrical power from the battery to turn the engine over, allowing the combustion process to begin.

Signs of a Failing Starter Motor

  • Clicking sound when starting the car
  • Engine does not crank
  • Intermittent starting issues
  • Lights turn on but engine won’t start
  • Burning smell near engine bay

Starter Motor Replacement Cost (UAE 2026)

Starter motor replacement typically costs between AED 400 – AED 1,800 depending on vehicle make and model.

When Should You Replace It?

You should replace the starter motor when starting becomes inconsistent or the motor completely fails to engage the engine.

Conclusion

A working starter motor ensures reliable engine ignition and is essential for everyday vehicle operation.
