What Carithm Does

Carithm provides a Decision-Support Refinement Layer for fleet maintenance. We quantify the internal structural health of automotive assets by bridging the gap between "Generic Manufacturer Guidelines" and "Real-World Operational Stress."

Rather than relying on static calendars or expensive hardware, Carithm uses Engineering Logic and Machine Learning to predict the depletion of a vehicle’s Designed Life.

Our Services

1. Sensorless Health % Diagnostics

We generate a Predictive Health Report for every vehicle in a fleet without requiring a single piece of OBD or IoT hardware. By ingesting raw service logs and telematics, we output a standardized Health %—a predictive risk metric that quantifies how much "buffer" remains before a component reaches its high-probability failure zone.

2. Regional Contextual Calibration

We apply Environmental Multipliers to standard maintenance schedules. Our system recognizes that a 10,000 km service interval in the 45°C heat and dust of the UAE (or the steep inclines of the Mediterranean) is physically far more taxing than the "average" laboratory conditions manufacturers use for their owner’s manuals. We adjust predictive analytics and algorithms based directly on regional service intervals and localized data.

3. Operational Asset Mapping

We provide a Unified Data Layer for modern fleet managers. We take fragmented, "dirty" data—messy spreadsheets and manual service logs—and map them against our comprehensive library of 500+ engine architectures. This gives service centers and managers a clear, prioritized list outlining which specific assets represent their highest absolute liability.
