The real risk behind repair quotes

Most workshops combine different types of work into a single list without clearly separating:

  • Confirmed faults
  • Wear-and-tear replacements
  • Preventative or precautionary work

When everything looks equally important, customers are forced to rely on trust instead of understanding.

8 Questions to clarify any repair quote

1. Is this routine maintenance or a fault repair?

2. What evidence supports this recommendation?

3. What happens if I delay this?

4. Is this part failing or just worn?

5. Are parts being bundled together unnecessarily?

6. Was this diagnosed or assumed from symptoms?

7. Is this safety-critical or optional?

8. Can I see labour vs parts breakdown?

Why this matters

A repair quote is not just a bill — it is a set of decisions. Each decision depends on evidence, interpretation, and risk level.

Without structured clarity, even fair quotes can feel uncertain.
