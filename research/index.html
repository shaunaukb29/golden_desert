Research Hub

Carithm Research publishes technical research and practical guides on vehicle maintenance, repair decisions, service history, and real-world component degradation. Our work spans fleet operators, independent workshops, and everyday vehicle owners across the UAE and beyond.

Rather than relying purely on manufacturer service schedules, our research explores how vehicles actually age under real operating conditions and how maintenance decisions can become more consistent, transparent, and data-driven.

Why this research exists

Manufacturer service schedules assume average operating conditions. Real vehicles rarely operate under average conditions.

Heat, dust, stop-start traffic, towing, heavy loads, and inconsistent maintenance change how components degrade throughout their lifecycle.

At the same time, workshops collect thousands of data points about every vehicle, while most owners leave with nothing more than an invoice. That information gap often becomes the source of uncertainty, unnecessary repairs, and inconsistent maintenance decisions.

Carithm Research exists to bridge that gap by studying how service history, component lifecycles, workshop policies, and operational conditions interact in the real world.

What we study

  • Vehicle degradation under real-world operating conditions
  • Manufacturer service intervals versus actual wear
  • Workshop maintenance decision systems
  • Repair quotation transparency
  • Service history interpretation
  • Predictive maintenance intelligence
  • Consumer decision support for vehicle repairs
