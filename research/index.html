Why this research exists

Most vehicle owners are not confused about price — they are confused about reasoning.

This research exists to translate workshop logic into clear decision structures so servicing and repairs can be understood before approval, not after cost escalation.

What we study

  • Workshop servicing decision logic
  • Repair recommendation validation
  • Service interval vs real-world wear mismatch
  • Inspection terminology interpretation
  • Brake and suspension diagnostic behaviour
  • Quote structure and bundling patterns
  • Consumer approval decision frameworks
  • AI-assisted repair estimate auditing
