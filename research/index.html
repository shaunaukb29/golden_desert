Research Hub

Carithm Research publishes technical research and practical guides on vehicle maintenance, repair quotation analysis, service history interpretation, and real-world component degradation. Our work spans fleet operators, independent workshops, and everyday vehicle owners across the UAE and beyond.

Rather than relying purely on manufacturer service schedules, our research explores how vehicles actually age under real operating conditions and how repair decisions are made inside workshops — often without transparent evidence for the customer.

Why this research exists

Manufacturer service schedules assume average operating conditions. Real vehicles rarely operate under average conditions.

Heat, dust, stop-start traffic, towing, heavy loads, and inconsistent maintenance change how components degrade throughout their lifecycle.

At the same time, workshops generate detailed inspection data, but customers often receive only a final invoice without supporting evidence. This creates a gap between what is observed and what is communicated.

Carithm Research exists to bridge that gap by translating workshop logic, service history, and mechanical wear patterns into transparent decision frameworks.

What we study

  • Vehicle degradation under real-world operating conditions
  • Manufacturer service intervals versus actual wear patterns
  • Workshop quotation and decision logic
  • Repair recommendation validation methods
  • Brake system repair justification patterns
  • Suspension failure diagnosis accuracy
  • Consumer repair decision support systems
