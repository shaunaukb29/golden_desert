Common Chevrolet Equinox Problems

10-Speed Automatic Transmission

Some higher-mileage vehicles develop transmission shudder or harsh shifting that may require fluid service or internal repairs.

Magnetic Ride Suspension

Models equipped with Magnetic Ride Control can require expensive damper replacement once mileage increases.

Air Conditioning Performance

Compressors and condensers experience heavy workloads in GCC summers, making AC repairs relatively common.

Dealer vs Independent Garage

Dealer servicing generally costs 30–50% more than experienced independent workshops. Independent specialists often provide comparable workmanship using OEM or genuine components.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Chevrolet Equinox expensive to maintain?

Maintenance costs are moderate for a full-size SUV, with major services generally costing AED 1,600–3,800.

What is the most common repair?

Suspension wear, transmission servicing, and air conditioning repairs are among the most common maintenance items.

Should I use a dealer?

Experienced independent garages are usually 30–50% cheaper while offering high-quality repairs.

How often should I service a Equinox?

Every 8,000–10,000 km is recommended for UAE driving conditions.
