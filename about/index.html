01 / WHY CARITHM EXISTS

Understanding your car
shouldn’t be complicated.

Finding out what’s wrong with your car usually means depending on someone else to tell you.

Diagnostic fees add up, and repair quotes can be hard to read. We think drivers deserve a clearer starting point.

Carithm listens to the sounds your car makes and turns them into plain-language insights, combining mechanical engineering with acoustic AI.

02 / THE PERSON BEHIND IT

One founder.
A lot of curiosity.

Shaunauk Basu
FOUNDER · 19 · MECHANICAL ENGINEERING STUDENT

“I’ve been into motorsport engineering for as long as I can remember. At 17 I started building Carithm because I thought finding out what’s wrong with a car could be a lot less confusing.”

Originally from Kolkata, Shaunauk now lives in the UAE, where he studies mechanical engineering and builds Carithm alongside his degree.

Carithm launched in 2025 and has stayed independent since, with no outside funding. Today it helps over 10,000 drivers a month, and it’s still a one-person project.
