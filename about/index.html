Engine Noise Check: What the AI Listens For

An engine noise check is the most common use of this tool. The AI performs an engine noise analysis by isolating the engine's acoustic signature from road and wind interference, then comparing it against fault patterns.

What an AI engine diagnosis covers

  • Knock and detonation — abnormal combustion events that produce a distinct metallic ping or thud, often under acceleration or load
  • Bearing noise — deep, rhythmic knocking that indicates rod or main bearing wear; position-dependent and RPM-linked
  • Timing chain rattle — a metallic rattle on cold start that may indicate chain stretch or tensioner wear
  • Accessory noise — belt squeal, alternator whine, AC compressor grinding, or power steering pump whine isolated from core engine noise
  • Valve train anomalies — ticking or tapping patterns that differ from normal mechanical valve noise
