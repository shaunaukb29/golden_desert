Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED.

Why do you need the engine number—isn't make/model enough? ▾
Make and model can share multiple engine variants with different part-life intervals. A Toyota Land Cruiser can carry a 1GR-FE or 3UR-FE—these engines have different timing chain wear curves, oil capacities, and service thresholds. The engine number locks the exact configuration so every prediction is accurate to that engine's spec, not an average across variants.
What if the service history is incomplete? ▾
Carithm works with whatever history is available. Where records are missing, our models apply conservative manufacturer-minimum intervals for that engine type, flagged clearly in the report. Partial history still produces actionable predictions—and often reveals components that are overdue based on mileage alone.
How is this different from just following the owner's manual? ▾
Owner's manuals give fixed calendar intervals that don't account for actual driving conditions, climate, or usage patterns. Carithm takes the actual replacement history and adjusts predicted remaining life against the real operating environment—city vs. highway, GCC heat vs. European climate. A brake pad replaced 3 years ago in Dubai has aged differently than the same pad in Amsterdam.
What components does a report cover? ▾
15+ components per report including brake pads (front/rear), brake fluid, fuel pump, battery, timing chain/belt, spark plugs, air filter, cabin filter, water pump, suspension bushings, oxygen sensors, and transmission fluid. The exact list adapts to the engine type and drivetrain configuration.
Does it work outside the UAE? ▾
Yes. Climate is one of the core inputs. The system tunes wear calculations for GCC heat, European winters, tropical humidity, and arid dusty environments. The engine number database covers global manufacturers and configurations.
What's the pricing model? ▾
Per-report pricing for service centers, monthly subscriptions for fleet operators. Volume pricing available. Contact us for a quote tailored to your operation size and region.
