Frequently asked questions

Can AI diagnose car problems?

AI can analyze vehicle sounds and combine them with OBD-II information to suggest possible causes. It is designed to help identify likely issues faster, but final repairs should always be confirmed by a qualified mechanic.

Can AI identify a car noise?

AI can analyze vehicle sound patterns and suggest possible causes. Carithm combines sound analysis with optional OBD-II information to provide additional context.

What is this car noise my vehicle is making?

Upload a recording of the sound and Carithm will analyze the audio to identify possible mechanical causes and recommended checks.

Can this be used across a whole fleet or multiple locations?

Yes. Since it runs in any browser with no installation, dispatch staff, drivers, or technicians at any location can record and submit a sound the same way — wherever your operation is based.

Does adding an OBD-II code actually improve accuracy?

It narrows the range of likely causes. Many mechanical noises — worn belts, bearing wear, suspension knock — never trigger a fault code, so sound alone often has to do the work. When a code is available, cross-referencing it against the sound gives a tighter, more specific read than either signal on its own.

Do our technicians need to install anything?

No. The diagnoser runs directly in a browser on any phone, tablet, or desktop — nothing to install or roll out across a team.

Is this built for individual car owners or for businesses?

The underlying tool works for either, but this page and workflow are set up for teams — fleets, workshops, dealerships, and rental operators — who are triaging noise complaints across more than one vehicle.

Which vehicle makes and years does it support?

It's built around mainstream makes and models used worldwide. If you run a specific fleet mix and want to check fit before rolling it out, reach out and we'll take a look.

Is there a cost for higher-volume business use?

The diagnoser is currently free to use. If you're looking at consistent, higher-volume use across a fleet or multiple branches, contact us directly and we'll talk through what that looks like.
