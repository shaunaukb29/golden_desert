FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I trust this report?

We don't inspect your car. We calculate whether a repair is expected based on engineering data, your mileage, the GCC climate, and manufacturer service intervals. If the numbers show a part has only used 35% of its expected wear life, you should ask your garage for a detailed explanation before approving the job.

Can this tell me if my repair price is too high?

The core analysis checks whether the repair is necessary at all — which is the bigger issue. If a part doesn't need replacing yet, the price is irrelevant. Where text structure matches known pricing benchmarks, the report will also flag suspicious regional pricing deviations.

Does it work for older or imported cars?

Yes. Our engineering database covers vehicles back to 2005, which includes most legacy models, common imports, and classic builds used across the GCC.

What do I do with the report after?

Take it to your garage. Use the questions it provides. A legitimate mechanic will welcome the conversation. One who gets defensive when you ask "why now?" is a red flag worth noticing.
