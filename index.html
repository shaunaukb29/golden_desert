FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between physical health and "designed life"?

A mechanic looks under the car and might claim a part looks worn just to secure a sale. Carithm looks at the underlying engineering data. We compute the mathematical lifespan of the part based on vehicle weight, drivetrain layout, mileage, and environment. If a part has only used 30% of its designed life, you should request a deep explanation from your garage before replacing it.

Can this app tell me if a specific repair price is too high?

The core engine checks the validity and necessity of the repair itself based on the part lifecycle. However, if the text structure matches common benchmarks, the output report highlights anomalies where regional pricing deviations look suspicious.

Does it work for old or imported cars?

Yes. Our historical engineering databases accommodate vehicles extending past 2005, which covers most legacy models, common imports, and classic builds operating in the GCC region.

Are you rolling out across the rest of the GCC?

Yes. While our primary regional environmental maps focus on the UAE right now, expansion modules specifically calibrated for atmospheric profiles and driving habits in **Qatar** and **Saudi Arabia** are scheduled to launch shortly.
