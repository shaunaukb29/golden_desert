Strategic Architecture FAQ

Why does the system show lower health percentages than basic telematics readers? +
Our system operates entirely on designed life metrics rather than guessing absolute mechanical failure. It calculates the exact remaining safety buffer engineered into a part based on environmental profiles and duty cycles. Showing the exhaustion of this safety margin allows fleets to plan systematically and workshops to upsell safely before a catastrophic highway breakdown occurs.
Is this technology bound to a specific global region? +
No. While our engine seamlessly accounts for extreme environmental multipliers (such as hot climate variables and intense idling profiles), the underlying physics models map standard mechanical component limits globally. It integrates effortlessly with any fleet system or workshop record pool anywhere in the world.
What raw inputs are required to run the models? +
We require zero system modifications. The system functions entirely by ingestion of existing vehicle records—such as basic engine log configurations, previous service timelines, and current mileage counts—running them through our proprietary AI degradation layers to extract clean, actionable yield indicators.
