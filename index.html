The Valuation Formula

Base Value = MSRP × Dep_Y1 × (Dep_Annual ^ (Age - 1))

Mileage Adjustment = -12% per 100k km over expected

100k+ Penalty = -10% if mileage > 100,000 km

Final Value = Base Value × Adjustments

All calculations specific to brand, model, trim, generation (Era), and exact configuration.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is your valuation different from Dubizzle or AutoTrader?

Those platforms show market estimates based on what people are asking for similar cars. We calculate actual depreciation from MSRP using engineering data—not market speculation.

How does predictive maintenance work without sensors?

We use vehicle metadata (engine code, mileage, age, drivetrain) combined with climate data and engineering-based wear curves to model component degradation. No OBD dongle or telematics hardware required.

What components can Carithm predict?

Most major service components including Brake Pads, Fuel Pump, Battery, Oxygen Sensor, Bushings, Tires, Water Pump, Air Filter, Timing Chain, Cabin Filter, Spark Plugs, and Brake Fluid. Additional components vary by engine type and manufacturer. All predictions factor in your local climate conditions.

Does it work in my region?

Valuation is UAE-specific (AED). Predictive maintenance adjusts for any climate globally—whether it's GCC heat, European cold, tropical humidity, or desert dust. We customize wear calculations for your specific operating environment.

Valuation tool is free. Predictive maintenance: we offer per-report pricing for service centers and monthly subscriptions for fleet operators. Contact us for a custom quote based on your volume and region.
