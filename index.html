Carithm AI: Sensor-Less Preventive Maintenance for Fleets & Service Centers

Identify potential issues across brakes, batteries, tires, and filters early. Use AI-driven maintenance insights to reduce downtime, plan service proactively, and prevent unexpected breakdowns.

Carithm AI is a sensor-less preventive maintenance and vehicle intelligence platform built for service centers and fleet operations. The system generates maintenance insights using structured inputs such as vehicle mileage, age, engine variant, historical part replacement intervals, basic vehicle profiles, driving behavior patterns, and regional climatic conditions—without relying on physical sensors.

Carithm AI combines machine-learning models trained on real-world component wear patterns with rule-based engineering logic to evaluate vehicle health and maintenance risk. The platform produces preventive maintenance reports covering critical components including brakes, batteries, tires, filters, engine oil, timing systems, and more.

The platform supports engine-specific analysis, allowing users to select the exact engine variant for each vehicle. Carithm AI currently supports 10 manufacturers and over 100 engines, using manufacturer-verified technical data to deliver accurate, engine-level maintenance recommendations.

Advanced engine-selection and enterprise capabilities are available upon request for partners and fleet-scale deployments.

Frequently Asked Questions About Carithm AI

Is Carithm a free AI car mechanic tool with no ads or signup?

Yes! Carithm offers a 100% free AI car diagnostic tool that helps you solve engine problems instantly. Get reliable guidance for your vehicle, just like having a virtual mechanic in your pocket — no sign-up, no ads.

Can I use Carithm to decode OBD-II codes like P0420, P0171, and P0300?

Absolutely! Carithm is a smart OBD-II code decoder. It explains the code, identifies likely causes (like catalytic converter or fuel system issues), and provides step-by-step DIY repair guidance for common engine problems.

How does Carithm diagnose issues like shaking at idle or battery drain?

The AI analyzes your symptoms and cross-references thousands of repair cases. It can pinpoint causes of shaking during idle (misfires, worn mounts) or detect battery drain and guide you with effective troubleshooting steps.

What is the Predictive Maintenance AI feature?

Carithm's Predictive Maintenance AI forecasts your car’s maintenance needs before problems occur. By analyzing mileage, vehicle age, driving style, and environment, it predicts which parts may require service or replacement soon — helping you prevent unexpected breakdowns and expensive repairs.

How can I try the Predictive Maintenance AI demo?

You can instantly test the free demo at carithm.vercel.app/predictive . Enter your vehicle details and get personalized predictions for parts like brakes, battery, tires, timing belts, and more.

Can Predictive Maintenance help me save money on repairs?

Yes! By knowing when parts are likely to fail, you can schedule maintenance ahead of time, avoid emergency repairs, and extend your car’s lifespan. This feature is especially useful for high-mileage, older, or imported vehicles.

Is Carithm available on mobile, or is it desktop-only?

Carithm works seamlessly on mobile, tablet, and desktop browsers — no app download required. Access both the AI Car Mechanic and Predictive Maintenance tools on any device, anytime.
