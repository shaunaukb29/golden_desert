Common AC and HVAC Noises

Air conditioners can make many different sounds depending on the component involved. Common noises include:

  • AC rattling noise
  • AC buzzing noise
  • AC humming noise
  • AC clicking noise
  • AC banging noise
  • AC knocking noise
  • AC grinding noise
  • AC squealing noise
  • AC hissing noise
  • AC whistling noise
  • AC vibrating noise
  • AC compressor noise
  • AC fan motor noise
  • HVAC blower noise

What Different AC Noises Can Mean

Rattling or vibrating: Rattling sounds can be associated with loose panels, debris, fan components, bearings or other mechanical issues.

Buzzing or humming: Unusual buzzing or humming can sometimes involve motors, compressors, capacitors, contactors or other electrical and mechanical components.

Clicking: Repeated clicking can occur when an AC system has difficulty starting or when electrical components such as contactors or relays are operating abnormally.

Hissing or bubbling: These sounds can sometimes be associated with refrigerant movement or a possible refrigerant leak. A sound alone cannot confirm a refrigerant leak, so professional testing may be required.

Squealing or grinding: High-pitched or grinding sounds may be associated with fan motors, bearings, blower components or other moving parts.

AC Compressor and Fan Sounds

The compressor and fan are two of the major moving components in an air conditioning system. Changes in their normal sound can sometimes indicate a mechanical or electrical problem. An AC sound recording can provide additional information about an unusual compressor, condenser fan, blower or motor noise.
