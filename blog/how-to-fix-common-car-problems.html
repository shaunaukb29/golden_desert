Top 10 Most Common
Car Problems

A masterclass guide on the most irritating, dangerous, and expensive automotive issues—and exactly how you can diagnose and fix them faster.

By Carithm Team • Jul 28, 2025 • 8 Min Read

If you own a car, you’ve almost certainly faced an irritating mechanical issue that disrupted your entire day—from aggressively shaking engines at a stoplight to cryptic warning lights randomly bleeding onto your dashboard.

Carithm helps you diagnose these exact problems with incredibly accurate AI analysis based on millions of real automotive data points. Before you panic and immediately book a stressful visit to the mechanic lock-shop, read through the top most common issues drivers encounter—or fire up Carithm AI right now for an instant analysis.

1 Engine Misfires

One of the most physically unsettling things that can happen while driving is feeling the entire engine block stumble, stutter, or jerk violently. Engine misfires almost always induce an incredibly rough idle, physical steering wheel shaking, and a catastrophic drop in performance outputs.

It is statistically bound to be caused by completely worn-out spark plugs, a shorting ignition coil, or severely clogged fuel injectors that choke the optimal air-fuel ratio. Swapping these parts out independently via scheduled tune-ups easily restores engine silkiness and avoids fatal catalytic converter damage.

2 Dead or Weak Battery

You hop in, turn the physical key or push your ignition button, and the car does absolutely nothing—or gives you the dreaded fast-click. This is the hallmark of a dangerously weak or entirely dead 12V battery. Over time, brutal summer heat, electrical leeching, or heavy corrosion accumulating on the lead terminals drastically suppresses cold cranking capabilities.

If you begin noticing your interior dome lights dimming aggressively or the radio cutting out on startup, schedule a battery replacement or run a swift alternator voltage test immediately.

3 Overheating Engine

The moment steam begins pouring dramatically out from under the front hood, or your dashboard temperature needle rapidly crosses over into the red zone, you are experiencing an overheating event. This is generally traced to critically low antifreeze coolant levels, massive radiators leaks, a seized water pump, or a thermostat that is permanently jammed shut.

Never ignore this warning—pull over instantly. Running an actively overheating engine for even five extra minutes can warp cylinder heads and total an engine block forever.

4 Brake Problems

Your brakes are unequivocally the most absolute critical safety component housed within your vehicle. If you suddenly hear intense, high-pitched squealing (indicative of worn brake pad wear-indicators rubbing against the rotor) or step on a dangerously soft, spongy brake pedal, it's time to pull over.

Do not defer brake maintenance; worn pads quickly score expensive brake rotors, and a squishy pedal means boiling brake fluid or dangerous oxygen pockets inside your hydraulic lines.

5 Transmission Issues

A flawless, buttery-smooth shifting transmission is imperative to your driving experience. If your automobile arbitrarily hesitates before shifting, 'slips' wildly out of gear while accelerating, or grinds loudly during engagement, your entire transmission system requires an immediate evaluation.

Often, a simple, comprehensive flush of the transmission fluid and filter swap resurrects failing gearboxes before catastrophic internal tooth decay occurs.

6 Check Engine Light (CEL)

It’s terrifyingly easy to ignore that glowing orange light—until a small stutter cascades into a massive engine stall. While the Check Engine Light frequently signals something as harmless as an improperly tightened gas cap, it can effortlessly mask severe hidden horrors, spanning oxygen sensor failures to rich-running misfires.

Leverage highly competent tools like Carithm. Vercel to instantly scan extracted OBD-II codes (like P0420) and definitively demystify exactly what is going on beneath your vehicle hood.

7 Starter Motor Failure

When you twist your ignition key and hear a lonely, singular 'click'—yet your electrical components operate perfectly—the physical starter motor has likely perished. This heavy-duty copper component strictly exists to spin the heavy engine flywheel to initiate combustion.

If the internal solenoid burns out or the mechanical gears strip away, zero amount of external battery jumping will breathe life into the vehicle.

8 Faulty Alternator

Your vehicle's alternator functions directly as a small-scale electrical generator, ceaselessly keeping the main battery charged and simultaneously powering complex vehicle electronics—from spark plugs to infotainment centers—while the primary engine runs.

If you're noticing severe interior light flickering, whining sounds from the drive belt, or an agonizingly slow engine-death while actively driving, the alternator unit is almost certainly failing. Replacing it rapidly completely ensures that your car doesn't completely die while you are driving in moving traffic.

9 Toxic Exhaust Smoke

Visually watching dense smoke erupting directly from your rear tailpipe is terrifying—but it communicates exactly what is wrong via a color-coded message.

  • Thick Blue Smoke: The engine is internally burning oil.
  • White Smoke: Vaporized coolant is actively leaking directly into the combustion chamber (Blown Head Gasket).
  • Black Smoke: The engine is running rich and inefficiently dumping excess, unburned fuel.

10 Bouncy Suspension Problems

If your daily driving experience has violently morphed from a smooth cruise into a jarring, teeth-chattering nightmare when contacting speed bumps or potholes, your suspension array demands attention.

A physically compromised suspension directly lengthens stopping distances during panic braking and allows the entire vehicle to dangerously 'lean' during heavy corner turns. Have your MacPherson struts, shock absorbers, and heavy-duty steel control arm bushings heavily inspected and successfully re-aligned.
