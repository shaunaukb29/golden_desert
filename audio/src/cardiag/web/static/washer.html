Why Is My Washing Machine Making Noise When Filling, Spinning or Draining?

Timing is basically your washing machine's alibi. When a noise shows up often narrows things down faster than what it actually sounds like, so here's what's typical at each stage of the cycle.

While it's filling

A brief hum, gurgle or buzz while water flows in is usually normal. A humming sound that continues without water actually flowing often points to the inlet valve or water supply rather than the machine itself.

While it's washing or agitating

A repeated clicking sound here, particularly one paired with the machine failing to start, is commonly linked to the door lock or lid switch rather than anything more dramatic.

During the spin cycle

This is where most of the alarming noises happen. Banging, thumping or a machine that "walks" across the floor usually means an unbalanced load or an unlevel machine. Grinding or squealing that gets louder over time more often points to worn bearings or a belt.

During or after draining

Gurgling or buzzing while water leaves the tub is usually part of normal operation. Humming with no spin during this stage can suggest a clutch, motor coupling or drive belt issue worth a closer look.

How to Record a Washing Machine Noise for AI Analysis

A clear recording can make it easier for an audio analysis tool, and any human you eventually call, to identify the characteristics of the sound instead of relying on your best verbal reenactment of a "thunk-thunk-squeak."

  • Record while the noise is happening. If the sound only happens during spinning, draining or agitation, record that part of the cycle.
  • Keep the phone reasonably close. Make sure the washing machine sound is louder than surrounding background noise.
  • Record continuously. Around 10–30 seconds can provide more useful context than a very short recording.
  • Avoid talking over the noise. Background speech can make the washing machine audio harder to analyze.
  • Don't put yourself in danger. Never open, disassemble or reach inside a machine while it is operating simply to obtain a recording.
