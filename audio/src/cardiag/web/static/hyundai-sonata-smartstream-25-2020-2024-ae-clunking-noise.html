1. Diagnostic Overview for Hyundai Sonata 2.5 (2020–2024) GCC

When a Hyundai Sonata 2.5 (2020–2024) GCC develops a clunking noise, it frequently signals broken front strut top mounts, torn lower control arm compliance bushings, or excessive driveshaft universal joint backlash.

Platform-Specific Advisory: On this vehicle, pay specific mechanical attention to Smartstream G2.5 oil consumption through piston rings and electronic parking brake actuator hum. Addressing the acoustic symptom early prevents cascading failures in neighboring engine or chassis subsystems.

2. Step-by-Step DIY Mechanical Inspection Checklist

Before taking your Hyundai Sonata 2.5 (2020–2024) GCC to a garage, perform these visual and physical checks safely:

  • Have an assistant bounce vehicle front end while holding top strut mount nut to feel for mechanical play.
  • Visually inspect control arm rear rubber bushings for deep rubber splits, tears, or oil contamination.
  • Shift transmission firmly between Drive and Reverse; a metallic clunk indicates driveshaft U-joint or motor mount slop.
  • Check subframe mounting bolts and front sway bar D-bushings for ovaled wear.

3. Estimated Repair Costs & Part Replacements

Standard workshop repairs for a clunking noise on a Hyundai Sonata 2.5 (2020–2024) GCC typically fall in the range of 400 – 2,170 AED.

Pricing varies based on genuine OEM vs. aftermarket component selection, mechanic labor rates, and whether related hardware (such as mounting bolts, seals, or tensioners) requires simultaneous renewal.
