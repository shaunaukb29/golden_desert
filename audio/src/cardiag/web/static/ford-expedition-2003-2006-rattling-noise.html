Step-by-Step DIY Diagnostic Checklist

Perform these physical checks safely before visiting an auto shop to isolate whether the issue is simple external hardware or internal mechanical wear:

  • ✓Inspect underside heat shields for rusted mounting brackets or loose clamp rings.
  • ✓Tap catalytic converter body with a rubber mallet (when cold) to listen for loose internal substrate.
  • ✓Check front and rear sway bar stabilizer links for torn rubber boots and physical play.
  • ✓Listen around the engine valve/timing cover on cold startup for timing chain rattle before oil pressure builds.
