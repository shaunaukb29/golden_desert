What Causes a Squeal When Turning?

Turning the steering wheel places a heavy load on the power steering pump. If your car has a hydraulic power steering system, the pump forces fluid through the rack to help turn the tires. This puts resistance on the power steering belt.

  • Loose Power Steering Belt: The most common cause. The extra load makes the belt slip over the pulley, causing a sharp squeal.
  • Low Power Steering Fluid: This usually sounds more like a "whine" or a "groan" than a pure squeal, getting louder the further you turn.
  • Suspension Ball Joints: Sometimes dry or failing lower ball joints emit a high-pitched squeak as the wheels pivot.

A Squeal vs A Whine

Belts squeal, pumps whine. If you aren't sure which one you are hearing, record the sound while safely turning the wheel back and forth in a parking lot, and the AI will classify it for you.
