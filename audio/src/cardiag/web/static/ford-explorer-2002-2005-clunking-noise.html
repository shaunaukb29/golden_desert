Before taking your 2002-2005 Ford Explorer to a mechanic, verify the source of the noise with these quick checks:

  1. Operating Scenario: Does the noise occur when driving over speed bumps, potholes, or shifting from park into drive? Note when it is loudest.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I drive my 2002-2005 Ford Explorer with this clunking noise?

If the noise is a minor heat shield rattle or accessory belt squeak, you can generally drive to a local shop. However, if you hear deep engine knocking, sudden loud grinding, or see a flashing Check Engine light, pull over immediately to prevent engine or transmission failure.
