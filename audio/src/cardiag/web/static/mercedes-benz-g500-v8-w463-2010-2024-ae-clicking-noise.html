1. Diagnostic Overview for Mercedes-Benz G 500 V8 (2010–2024) GCC

When a Mercedes-Benz G 500 V8 (2010–2024) GCC develops a clicking sound, it typically occurs when outer constant velocity (CV) axle boots tear and lose grease, or when dashboard HVAC blend door actuators strip plastic gears.

Platform-Specific Advisory: On this vehicle, pay specific mechanical attention to front suspension panhard rod bush play causing steering shimmy and door latch seal wind noise at 140 km/h. Addressing the acoustic symptom early prevents cascading failures in neighboring engine or chassis subsystems.

2. Step-by-Step DIY Mechanical Inspection Checklist

Before taking your Mercedes-Benz G 500 V8 (2010–2024) GCC to a garage, perform these visual and physical checks safely:

  • Perform a slow full-lock turn in an empty parking lot; rhythmic clicking on tight turns confirms outer CV joint failure.
  • Look behind front wheels for torn accordion CV rubber boots and flung black axle grease.
  • Listen behind dashboard when changing temperature or air recirculation; rapid clicking confirms stripped blend door gear.
  • Check brake caliper guide pins and anti-rattle clips for missing hardware or loose fit.

3. Estimated Repair Costs & Part Replacements

Standard workshop repairs for a clicking sound on a Mercedes-Benz G 500 V8 (2010–2024) GCC typically fall in the range of 295 – 1,760 AED.

Pricing varies based on genuine OEM vs. aftermarket component selection, mechanic labor rates, and whether related hardware (such as mounting bolts, seals, or tensioners) requires simultaneous renewal.
