1. Diagnostic Overview for Peugeot e-208 EV (2019–2024) EU

When a Peugeot e-208 EV (2019–2024) EU develops a squealing noise, it usually caused by glazed or slipping serpentine accessory belts, failing idler pulleys, seized alternator decoupler bearings, or brake wear indicators.

Platform-Specific Advisory: On this vehicle, pay specific mechanical attention to OBC 11kW integrated onboard charger failure and air conditioning heat pump compressor rattle. Addressing the acoustic symptom early prevents cascading failures in neighboring engine or chassis subsystems.

2. Step-by-Step DIY Mechanical Inspection Checklist

Before taking your Peugeot e-208 EV (2019–2024) EU to a garage, perform these visual and physical checks safely:

  • Check serpentine belt ribs for glazing, micro-cracking, or rubber rib separation.
  • Observe belt tensioner arm while engine idles to check for erratic bouncing or damper failure.
  • Test whether squeal occurs when applying brakes (brake wear tab) vs. when turning steering wheel (power steering slip).
  • Briefly spray a tiny mist of clean water onto the ribbed belt surface to see if the chirp momentarily stops.

3. Estimated Repair Costs & Part Replacements

Standard workshop repairs for a squealing noise on a Peugeot e-208 EV (2019–2024) EU typically fall in the range of 70 € – 350 €.

Pricing varies based on genuine OEM vs. aftermarket component selection, mechanic labor rates, and whether related hardware (such as mounting bolts, seals, or tensioners) requires simultaneous renewal.
