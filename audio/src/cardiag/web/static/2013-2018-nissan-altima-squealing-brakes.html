Common Causes of Squealing in a 2013-2018 Nissan Altima

Owners of this generation frequently report squealing noises tied to the brakes system. Here's what's most commonly behind it:

  • Pad glazing from light city driving: A squeal at low speed that reduces or disappears under firmer, harder braking.
  • Wear indicator contact: A consistent metallic squeal on every stop signaling pads are near replacement.
  • Rust film on rotors after sitting: A squeal on the first few stops of the day that fades after normal driving.
  • Loose caliper hardware: An intermittent squeal combined with a light vibration through the brake pedal.

FAQ: 2013-2018 Nissan Altima Squealing Noise

Why do my Altima's brakes squeal in the morning but not later?

Rust film commonly forms on rotors overnight, especially in humid climates, and that film is what squeals off during the first few stops of the day.

Is this squeal something to worry about right away?

Squeal alone usually isn't urgent, but grinding or a soft/vibrating pedal would be, so it's worth checking pad thickness if the squeal has been going on for a while.

Does city driving really wear brakes differently?

Frequent light, low-speed braking in stop-and-go traffic is a well-known cause of pad glazing, which produces noise without necessarily wearing the pad material down quickly.

Will new pads alone fix a squeal, or do I need rotors too?

It depends on rotor condition — a shop can usually tell from thickness and surface wear whether pads alone will resolve it or if resurfacing is needed too.
