Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I pay for this repair on a Honda Civic?

A fair price at a reputable independent repair shop is approximately $264. Dealerships typically charge around $418 due to higher labor rates and OEM packaging markups.

How do I know which wheel bearing is bad?

A bad wheel bearing hums louder when you steer gently in the direction that loads that side (e.g., turning right loads the left bearing, making it louder).

Can a bad wheel bearing cause vibration in the steering wheel?

Yes; worn bearing balls allow excess play in the hub assembly, causing steering shimmy and uneven tire tread wear.

Is it safe to drive with a noisy wheel bearing?

Driving with a growling bearing is dangerous; prolonged heat friction can weld the bearing seized or shear the spindle, causing wheel detachment.
