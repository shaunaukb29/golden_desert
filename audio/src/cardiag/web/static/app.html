How AI Engine Sound Analysis Works

  1. Record the unusual sound using your phone — hold it close to the noise source.
  2. Upload the recording through the tool above.
  3. The AI removes background noise and extracts acoustic features.
  4. Machine learning models compare your recording against learned mechanical sound patterns.
  5. You get a ranked list of likely fault areas and a confidence score.
  6. If recording quality is insufficient, the system says so — it never invents a diagnosis.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free app to diagnose car noises?

Yes — Carithm's AI Engine Sound Analyzer is free with no signup required. Upload any unusual car noise recording and get an instant AI analysis identifying likely fault areas.

How do I diagnose a car noise in the UAE?

Record the noise on your phone, then upload it here. The tool identifies likely fault locations and tells you what to have inspected, so you go into any Dubai or Abu Dhabi garage already knowing what's likely wrong.

Can AI identify engine knocking?

Yes. Engine knock, detonation, ticking, rattling, squealing, and many other abnormal mechanical sounds can often be identified depending on recording quality.

Can I record using my phone?

Yes. Most smartphone recordings are sufficient. Hold the phone close to the noise source, reduce cabin and wind noise where possible, and the analyzer will handle the rest.

Does background noise affect accuracy?

The system automatically reduces background noise and flags when interference is too high for a reliable result — it never invents a diagnosis when the recording is unclear.

Does the AI always make a prediction?

No. When evidence is insufficient, the platform reports uncertainty rather than generating unsupported conclusions.
