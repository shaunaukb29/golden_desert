Understanding Common Diagnostic Results

If you're wondering what the AI might find, here's a breakdown of the acoustic signatures our car noise identifier looks for when assessing your car.

The Meaning Behind High-Pitched Squeals

A sharp squeal during a cold start or heavy acceleration usually indicates a rubber component failing to grip a metal pulley — typically a serpentine or accessory belt. If the squeal occurs when applying the brake pedal, it's almost certainly the acoustic wear indicator on your brake pads warning you that it's time for a replacement.

Decoding Heavy Knocks and Clunks

Low-frequency knocking sounds require immediate attention. An engine knock that increases with RPM can signal dangerous detonation or worn internal bearings. On the other hand, if a clunk only happens when you hit a pothole, the diagnoser will likely point to your suspension system, such as a worn strut mount or control arm bushing.

Identifying Whines and Hums

A steady hum that grows louder as you accelerate often points to a wheel bearing approaching failure. If the sound is more of a high-pitched whine that changes with engine speed (even in park), the AI will usually flag power steering pumps, alternators, or a stressed transmission.

Tracking Down Rattles and Vibrations

Rattling sounds are notoriously difficult for humans to locate because they echo underneath the chassis. The AI Car Diagnoser can distinguish between the light metallic rattle of a loose exhaust heat shield and the deeper rattle of a failing catalytic converter honeycomb.
