How to Pinpoint Your Car Sound in 4 Steps

  1. Identify the operating conditions: Does the sound change with engine RPM (neutral rev test) or with vehicle wheel speed (coasting in neutral)? RPM-tied noises are in the engine/accessories; speed-tied noises are in the drivetrain, brakes, or wheel bearings.
  2. Check under the hood visually: Check engine oil dipstick level and look for dangling heat shields before driving further.
  3. Record a clean audio sample: Keep windows slightly cracked and phone held steady away from direct cabin AC vents.
  4. Run the AI audio diagnosis: Upload the sample into the free analyzer above to receive a confidence-scored breakdown.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a smartphone microphone accurately capture engine sounds?

Yes. Modern smartphone microphones feature wide dynamic range capabilities. Our acoustic AI filters ambient wind turbulence and background city noise to focus purely on high-frequency metal-on-metal impacts and low-frequency mechanical vibrations.

What is the most dangerous car noise to ignore?

A deep metallic engine knocking sound (rod knock). Driving even a few miles with a failing rod bearing can cause the connecting rod to puncture the engine block, destroying the entire engine.
