Why Does It Only Squeal Under Acceleration?

When you press the gas pedal, the engine RPM increases rapidly. This puts immediate rotational force (torque) on the serpentine belt and the accessories it drives. If there is a weakness in the system, this sudden load will cause a slip or a bearing to complain.

  • Slipping Serpentine Belt: The belt cannot grip the pulleys firmly enough to match the engine's acceleration.
  • Failing Tensioner: The spring mechanism that keeps the belt tight has worn out, allowing slack under load.
  • Alternator or Water Pump Drag: An accessory component is failing and resisting rotation, forcing the belt to drag across its pulley.
