1. Diagnostic Overview for Mitsubishi Pajero 3.8 V6 Signature (2007–2021) GCC

When a Mitsubishi Pajero 3.8 V6 Signature (2007–2021) GCC develops a rattling noise, it typically points to loose exhaust heat shields, degraded catalytic converter ceramic cores, timing chain slack, or worn suspension stabilizer links.

Platform-Specific Advisory: On this vehicle, pay specific mechanical attention to 6G75 valve guide seal oil burning, rear trailing arm bush delamination, and dashboard sun cracking. Addressing the acoustic symptom early prevents cascading failures in neighboring engine or chassis subsystems.

2. Step-by-Step DIY Mechanical Inspection Checklist

Before taking your Mitsubishi Pajero 3.8 V6 Signature (2007–2021) GCC to a garage, perform these visual and physical checks safely:

  • Inspect underside heat shields for rusted mounting brackets or loose clamp rings.
  • Tap catalytic converter body with a rubber mallet (when cold) to listen for loose internal substrate.
  • Check front and rear sway bar stabilizer links for torn rubber boots and physical play.
  • Listen around the engine valve/timing cover on cold startup for timing chain rattle before oil pressure builds.

3. Estimated Repair Costs & Part Replacements

Standard workshop repairs for a rattling noise on a Mitsubishi Pajero 3.8 V6 Signature (2007–2021) GCC typically fall in the range of 330 – 2,390 AED.

Pricing varies based on genuine OEM vs. aftermarket component selection, mechanic labor rates, and whether related hardware (such as mounting bolts, seals, or tensioners) requires simultaneous renewal.
