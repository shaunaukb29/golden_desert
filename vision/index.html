Understanding Your Repair Estimates

Keep these industry factors in mind when reviewing your repair costs:

  • Out-of-pocket vs. Insurance pricing: Shops may offer different rates depending on who is paying the final bill. Always ask if there is a cash or out-of-pocket discount.
  • Paint blending costs: The dent itself is often cheaper to fix than the paint. Matching modern metallic or pearl finishes usually requires blending paint into adjacent panels, significantly increasing the cost.
  • OEM vs. Aftermarket parts: Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) parts are more expensive than aftermarket alternatives. Verify which type of part is being quoted in your repair estimate.
  • Total Loss Thresholds: Insurers often declare a car totaled if repair costs exceed 70–80% of its cash value. This is a financial threshold, not necessarily an indicator that the car is mechanically unrepairable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this repair estimate tool free?

Yes, it is completely free with no account or signup required.

Can I use this for my auto insurance claim?

This AI assessment is a visual aid and baseline for understanding repair costs before visiting an auto body shop. It is not a certified mechanic's inspection and cannot be officially submitted as an insurance adjuster's appraisal.

How much does it cost to fix a minor dent or scratch?

A minor door dent typically costs $75 to $250 with paintless dent repair. Deep scratches or dents that crack the paint can run $300 to $900 at a body shop.

Should I file an insurance claim for minor auto body damage?

If the repair estimate is close to or below your deductible, paying out of pocket is usually cheaper. Filing a claim for minor damage can raise your insurance premium for several years.

What happens if you damage a rental car?

Rental agreements bill you directly for new damage found at return unless covered by a damage waiver, personal auto insurance, or credit card protection. Taking photos and using an AI scanner before return helps document the vehicle's condition.
